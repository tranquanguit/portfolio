## The title

"Sinh viên 5 tốt" — Student with 5 Good Criteria — is a title of the Vietnam Students' Association, awarded across five dimensions:

1. **Good morality** — conduct and contribution to the student community.
2. **Good study** — academic results above a defined threshold.
3. **Good physical fitness** — participation in sport and physical activity.
4. **Good volunteering** — participation in volunteer campaigns and community programs.
5. **Good integration** — foreign language capability, and participation in international or cross-cultural activity.

I received it at university level in **2021 and 2022**.

## Why it is on a technical portfolio

Because it is the only line on my CV that measures something other than output.

The five criteria are deliberately hard to satisfy simultaneously — strong grades alone are not enough, and neither is being the person who shows up to every volunteer campaign. Holding all five in the same year, twice, required actually planning a year rather than reacting to it.

That is the habit it built, and it is the one I still use: deciding at the start of a period what the small number of things that matter are, and then protecting the time for them.

## The volunteering and integration parts

These two mattered most in the long run. Volunteering is where I met people outside my faculty and outside computer science, which is most of the reason I ended up comfortable talking to business users rather than only to developers. Integration is where I started taking English seriously — five years before it became the working language of a client engagement in the Philippines.
